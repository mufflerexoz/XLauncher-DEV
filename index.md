# XLauncher Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026 · Developer: mufflerexoz_

XLauncher is a home-screen launcher. It does **not** collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

## Data stored on your device only
- Your local profile (username and optional avatar picture), wallpaper choice and launcher settings.
- The list of apps you open from XLauncher, when, and for how long (used for "Jump back in" and the launch page).
- Images you pick for avatars, wallpapers, tiles, icons and banners are copied into the app's private storage.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall XLauncher or clear its storage.

## Permissions
- **Play time**: measured by XLauncher itself (time between starting a game from XLauncher and returning to it).
  No Usage access permission is used. Stored on your device only.
- **Query installed apps**: needed to list and launch your apps.
- **Network state**: shows the Wi-Fi/Ethernet status icon on Android TV.
- **Internet** (optional artwork search): only used when you enter your own API key for SteamGridDB, TheGamesDB,
  IGDB or Google. XLauncher then sends the **game's name** to that service to find artwork and downloads the
  images. Your API keys are stored on your device only. No other data is sent.
- **Photos** (optional): XLauncher has no access to your photos. When you choose a wallpaper, avatar, tile, icon or
  banner, Android's photo picker gives XLauncher only the one picture you select, which is copied into
  XLauncher's private storage. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Folders you choose** (ROM folders): read only to list your games; nothing is uploaded.
- **Delete packages request**: only used when you choose "Uninstall" for an app.

## Purchases
Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. XLauncher receives only the purchase status from
Google Play Billing; payment details are handled by Google under its own privacy policy.
